### Executive Summary

Vriksha Organics produces premium eco-friendly home care and kitchen consumables. While consumer demand was strong, their manufacturing cash cycle was severely constrained by 45-day payment settlement cycles from marketplace aggregators and unrecovered return deductions.

### The Problem

- **Working Capital Lockup**: Over ₹35 Lakhs was tied up in unpaid marketplace remittances and delayed payout cycles.
- **RTO & Return Deduction Leakage**: Marketplace deduction reports were so complicated that Vriksha lacked the team bandwidth to dispute invalid customer return claims.
- **Packaging Compliance Penalties**: Flipkart and Amazon fulfillment centers repeatedly flagged barcode alignment and carton dimension issues, imposing inbound rejection fees.

### The Solution with Social Technologies

- **1P Transition via ICM Retails**: Vriksha shifted to a wholesale 1P partnership. ICM Retails bought finished goods directly on 15-day payment terms, completely relieving Vriksha of debtor risk.
- **Automated Claims Recovery**: Social Technologies' automated audit engine cross-checked all return files against courier manifest receipts, filing automated dispute claims for damaged and swapped items.
- **Hub-Level Inbound Compliance**: Standardized master carton packaging according to Amazon FBA and Flipkart FC specifications.

### The Results

- **Immediate Cash Flow Stability**: Working capital turnaround improved by 30 days, enabling a 2x factory output increase.
- **₹8.4 Lakhs in Recovered Deductions**: Successfully reclaimed unjustified marketplace deductions within 90 days.
- **Expansion to ₹1.4 Cr Run-Rate**: Increased SKU catalog presence from 18 items to 65 active listings with Prime and Assured tags.
