### Executive Summary

Apex Luggage built a solid reputation on Amazon for durable poly-carbonate suitcases. However, their reliance on a single channel left them vulnerable to sudden fee hikes and ad saturation. They wanted to tap into high-growth tier-2 and tier-3 markets through Meesho and fashion buyers on Myntra without degrading brand perception.

### The Problem

- **Tiered Channel Segmentation**: Fear that low-ASP regional marketplace selling would cannibalize premium Amazon Prime sales.
- **High Logistics Return Rates (RTO)**: Non-delivery rates in smaller towns frequently reached 32% on cash-on-delivery orders.
- **Fragmented Order Routing**: 4 disparate warehouse panels resulted in manual dispatch bottlenecks and missed SLA cutoffs.

### The Solution with Social Technologies

- **Sub-Brand Catalog Stratification**: Segmented the product line into core premium lines for Amazon/Myntra and high-volume entry-level packs for Meesho and Flipkart.
- **NDR (Non-Delivery Report) Automation**: Automated real-time WhatsApp verification for high-risk COD orders prior to dispatch, confirming customer presence and address accuracy.
- **Unified 3PL Carrier Selection**: Integrated intelligent carrier routing picking optimal line-haul nodes (Delhivery Surface vs. Ekart) based on regional PIN performance history.

### The Results

- **RTO Reduced from 32% to 14.5%**: Massive cost savings across high-volume regional dispatches.
- **4.8x Blended ROAS**: Coordinated seasonal campaigns across Myntra Big Fashion Festival and Amazon Diwali sales.
- **1,850 Units Dispatched Daily**: Seamlessly handled holiday peak velocity across 4 connected marketplace channels.
