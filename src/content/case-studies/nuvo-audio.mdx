### Executive Summary

Nuvo Lifestyle, a fast-emerging Indian smart audio and wearable accessories brand, was growing rapidly on Amazon India but facing severe operational bottlenecks. Peak sale events like Flipkart Big Billion Days and Amazon Great Indian Festival frequently triggered ghost-inventory out-of-stock strikes, resulting in ₹12 Lakhs in platform SLA penalties and lost Buy Box share to unauthorized reseller hijackers.

### The Problem

1. **Inventory Buffer Desync**: A 15-minute sync delay between their warehouse ERP and marketplace seller portals led to 80+ oversold units per week.
2. **Buy Box Hijacking**: Gray-market sellers undercut prices by ₹20 to ₹50 during midnight flash events, stealing the Buy Box for 6+ hours before the Nuvo team noticed.
3. **Ad Spend Cannibalization**: Uncoordinated bids across identical keywords on Amazon and Flipkart inflated CPC by 38% without increasing incremental organic ranking.

### The Solution with Social Technologies

- **Sub-Second Buffer Locking**: Nuvo integrated directly into our central orchestration terminal. Real-time 2-way webhooks reduced multi-portal stock updates to under 800 milliseconds.
- **Algorithmic Buy Box Repricing**: Deployed dynamic rule-based pricing algorithms that match competing offers within 60 seconds while enforcing hard floor contribution margins.
- **Unified Catalog & Sponsored Ads**: Re-architected Nuvo's A+ listings across 48 SKUs and deployed algorithmic dayparting on Amazon Sponsored Products and Flipkart PLA.

### The Results

- **+310% GMV Velocity**: Total monthly revenue scaled from ₹28 Lakhs to ₹1.15 Crores within six months.
- **97.2% Buy Box Retention**: Sustained dominance against unauthorized seller listings.
- **Zero SLA Cancellation Penalties**: 100% on-time dispatch SLA maintained across Delhivery and ATS courier hubs.
- **4.82x Blended ROAS**: Lowered blended acquisition cost by 26% while doubling daily unit dispatch volume.
